privacy

This is the website of the film Los Batracios (The Batrachians). Here we explain what data we collect and why. Last updated: October 13, 2026.

Controller

Diego Salomon. Contact: [email protected].

Visit statistics

We count visits with Umami, a tool that uses no cookies and stores no data that identifies you. We only see totals: approximate country and city, the page you came from, device type, browser, language and which buttons get clicked. We do this to learn what works in promoting the film (legitimate interest). The servers are in the European Union and this data is deleted after 25 months at most.

Mailing list

If you leave us your email, we use it only to send you news about the film, such as screenings and releases. The legal basis is your consent. The list is managed by Buttondown, which may store data outside the European Union with the safeguards the law requires. We keep your email until you unsubscribe. Every email has a link to do so. To stop bots, the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks technical connection data (such as IP address and browser) only for that purpose and uses no advertising cookies.

Links to other websites

Links to YouTube, Letterboxd, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and X take you to those websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its use, restrict it or get a copy by writing to [email protected]. If you think we handle it badly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
